About the educator

Dr Meet Joshi

Welcome to MedEduWithDrMeet — a medical education platform focused on making health information understandable and accessible.

Read the full professional profile →

Dr Meet Joshi holds an MBBS degree and brings clinical experience from 8 months as a Junior Resident in the ICU at Banas Medical College and Research Institute, Palanpur, followed by 3 years of experience as a Medical Officer.

He is currently working as a Medical Officer and continues to create educational content on medical concepts, laboratory investigations and public health with a practical, learner-friendly approach.
