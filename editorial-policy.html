Editorial Policy

How MedEduWithDrMeet plans, researches, writes, reviews and corrects educational medical content.

Our editorial purpose

MedEduWithDrMeet aims to make medical concepts, laboratory investigations and public-health information easier to understand for learners and the wider community. Content is educational, accessible and intended to encourage informed conversations with healthcare professionals.

Who creates the content

The platform is created and presented by Dr Meet Joshi, MBBS, Medical Officer and medical educator. Professional background is described on the About page. The presence of a medical qualification does not mean every article has undergone independent peer review.

Research and source selection

When preparing medical content, we aim to prioritise reliable and relevant sources, such as:

  • Guidelines and publications from recognised public-health and professional organisations, including WHO and relevant national health authorities.
  • Peer-reviewed medical literature and established clinical references.
  • Official medicine and vaccine information from appropriate regulatory or public-health bodies.

Sources should be appropriate to the topic and the claims being discussed. Where practical, articles should identify useful references or further-reading resources. Content should not rely on promotional claims or unsupported social-media posts as medical evidence.

Accuracy, clarity and balance

  • We aim to distinguish established evidence from uncertainty, emerging research and opinion.
  • Risks, limitations and important context should be explained alongside potential benefits.
  • Medical terms should be explained in accessible language without intentionally distorting their meaning.
  • Drug doses, treatment regimens and clinical recommendations require particular care and should be checked against current authoritative guidance before publication.
  • Educational content should not encourage self-medication, unsafe treatment or delay in seeking appropriate care.

Updates and corrections

Medical knowledge can evolve. We aim to correct material errors and update content when important guidance changes or a credible correction is brought to our attention. Updated articles may be revised to improve clarity, accuracy or relevance. If you notice a possible error, please send the article link and a brief explanation through our Contact page.

We will assess correction requests against reliable evidence. Contacting us does not guarantee a particular change, but relevant concerns will be considered.

Independence and conflicts of interest

Educational content should be prepared to inform rather than to promote a product, medicine, laboratory or service. Any material sponsorship, paid partnership or relevant conflict associated with a specific item should be clearly disclosed where applicable. No endorsement should be inferred merely because a product or organisation is mentioned for educational context.

Images, privacy and patient confidentiality

We aim to respect copyright, image permissions and individual privacy. Patient-identifiable information, confidential medical records or images should not be published without an appropriate lawful basis and valid consent. Clinical examples should be anonymised and should not expose a patient's identity.

Editorial limitations

The content is not represented as a substitute for formal peer review, institutional clinical guidance or individual medical care. Readers should consult current guidelines and qualified professionals for decisions affecting patient care.
