Why MedEduWithDrMeet?

Medical information can feel complicated. This platform aims to make important concepts easier to follow for students, healthcare learners and the general public. Topics include medical concepts, laboratory investigations, disease awareness, prevention and public health.

The goal is to encourage informed conversations and responsible health awareness—not self-diagnosis or self-medication.
